Our Story

Tembea was created to help people discover the beauty and diversity of Kenya. From the wildlife of the national parks to the beaches of the Coast, Kenya offers experiences for travellers with different interests and budgets.

Our goal is to make discovering these destinations simple, accessible and inspiring. Tembea brings together useful information about destinations, travel seasons and estimated daily budgets so that visitors can make informed decisions when planning their trips.

We believe that travelling is more than visiting new places. It is an opportunity to experience different cultures, appreciate nature and create lasting memories. Tembea aims to encourage both local and international visitors to explore Kenya responsibly.

"We created Tembea because Kenya has incredible places waiting to be explored, and everyone should have an easy way to discover them."

— Amina Hassan, Founder of Tembea

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Tembea?

Tembea is a travel discovery platform that helps visitors explore destinations across Kenya and plan their trips.

Which destinations can I discover on Tembea?

Tembea features destinations across Kenya, including Nairobi, Nakuru, Mombasa and the Kenyan Coast, with more destinations to be added.

Can Tembea help me plan my trip?

Yes. You can explore our destination information and use the contact page to send us a trip inquiry with your preferred travel date and travel style.

Does Tembea arrange accommodation and transport?

Tembea is primarily a destination discovery platform. Specific accommodation and transport arrangements can be discussed when submitting a trip inquiry.
